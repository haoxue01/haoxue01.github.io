Hao Xue

Assistant Professor, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), China
AI Thrust and Urban Governance and Design Thrust (joint appointment)
Senior Visiting Fellow, University of New South Wales, Australia

Hi! I am an Assistant Professor at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), with a joint appointment in the AI Thrust (Information Hub) and the Urban Governance and Design Thrust (Society Hub). I am also an Affiliated Assistant Professor at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, and a co-founder of Data Cruiser, a Sydney-based spin-out built on our research.

Before joining HKUST(GZ) in February 2026, I was a Lecturer in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of New South Wales (UNSW Sydney), Australia, and before that a Research Fellow at UNSW Sydney and RMIT University. I am an Associate Investigator of the ARC Centre of Excellence for Automated Decision-Making and Society (ADM+S) and co-lead its GenAISim signature project.

My research sits at the intersection of AI and ubiquitous, urban systems: spatiotemporal data representation and reasoning, foundation models for multimodal and dynamic environments, large language models for time series and sensor data, and open-world learning and reasoning. Applications include transportation and human mobility, energy and buildings, public health, and wearable and IoT sensing.

I obtained my PhD in Computer Science from The University of Western Australia in 2020. Before that, I obtained my bachelor's and master's degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2014 and 2016.
